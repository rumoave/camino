Frequently asked questions

What is Camino?

An educational study and preparation app for the U.S. immigration journey — civics test prep, your legal path, interview practice, and document organization, in English and Spanish. Camino is not a law firm and does not give legal advice.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Camino Plus starts with a 7-day free trial. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, it renews at the price shown on the paywall. You can cancel any time from your device: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Camino.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open Camino, go to the upgrade screen, and tap Restore purchases. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?

Your study progress is stored on your device, so deleting the app removes it. Your subscription can be restored (see above), but local progress does not transfer between devices in this version.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your information stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is Camino affiliated with USCIS?

No. Camino is an independent educational app and is not affiliated with USCIS or any government agency.

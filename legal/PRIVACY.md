# Privacy Policy — Camino

_Last updated: July 13, 2026_

> Hosted version (the URL App Store Connect points to):
> https://rumoave.github.io/camino/privacy.html — served from the `gh-pages` branch.

Camino ("we," "us") is an educational study and preparation app for the U.S.
immigration journey. This policy explains what data the app handles. We designed
Camino to keep your information on your device.

## What we collect

**On your device only.** Your profile, study progress, streak, documents checklist,
and settings are stored **locally on your device** (in the app's storage). This
information does not leave your device and is not sent to us. We do not operate
servers that receive it.

**We do not collect:** your name, email, contact info, location, browsing activity,
or any analytics that identify you. Camino has no user accounts in this version.

## Subscriptions

Camino offers an optional paid subscription ("Camino Plus") with a free trial.
Payments are processed by **Apple** through the App Store — we never see or store
your payment details.

To manage your subscription entitlement, we use **RevenueCat**, a subscription
infrastructure provider. RevenueCat processes your purchase status and an
anonymous app-user identifier to keep your subscription working across your
devices. It does not receive your name or contact information from us. See
RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## How your information is used

Only to make the app work for you: to save your progress on your device and to
unlock Camino Plus features if you subscribe.

## Sharing

We do **not** sell or share your personal information with third parties. The only
third parties involved are Apple (payments) and RevenueCat (subscription status),
as described above.

## Tracking

We do **not** track you across other apps or websites. Camino does not use
advertising identifiers.

## Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, you can remove it any time from
**Me → Clear all data**, or by deleting the app.

## Children

Camino is a general-audience educational app and is not directed to children
under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
